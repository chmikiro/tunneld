Privacy Policy

Tunnel'd does not collect, store, or share any personal or sensitive user data externally. All data stays on your device unless you explicitly export it.

Data Processing — Third-Party Services

Tunnel'd contacts the following services to function. Each request inherently includes your device's IP address as part of the TCP connection:

  • ipify (ipify.org) — determines your public IP address. ipify states they do not store or track request data. [Privacy Policy]
  • ip2location (ip2location.io) — geolocation (country, city, ISP, ASN, coordinates, timezone). [Privacy Policy]
  • ipapi.co (ipapi.co) — alternative geolocation provider. [Privacy Policy]
  • ipinfo.io (ipinfo.io) — alternative geolocation provider. [Privacy Policy]
  • ipdia.li (ipdia.li) — DNS leak test. Runs on self-hosted infrastructure with no third-party analytics or tracking.

Which geolocation provider is used depends on your selection in Settings → Geo IP Data Source.

By default, you can use Tunnel'd without any external network access — the app will still display your IP address and network type as detected by Android's system APIs.

Local Data Storage

Tunnel'd stores your IP address history locally on the device using an on-device database. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to export it (CSV export to a location of your choice) or clear it (Settings → Clear History).

No Analytics, No Telemetry

Tunnel'd contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics frameworks, no crash reporters, and no telemetry of any kind. It is a standalone app — no account required, no backend to phone home to.

Consent

By using Tunnel'd, you consent to your IP address being sent to the third-party services listed above solely for the purpose of IP address and geolocation lookups. You may opt out of geolocation lookups entirely by skipping the enrichment step or using the app offline.

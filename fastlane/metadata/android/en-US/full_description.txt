Tunnel'd is a no-bullshit IP address tracker and DNS leak tester. No accounts, no analytics, no telemetry — your data stays on your device.

Features:
• See your public IP (v4 and v6) at a glance
• DNS leak test to verify your VPN is actually working
• History of your IP addresses with rich geolocation details
• Filter by IP version, network type (Wi-Fi/Cellular/VPN), and country
• Export and import your history as CSV
• Copy selected fields from any IP entry
• Dark/light/system theme support
• Background notifications when your IP changes
• Zero third-party analytics or tracking SDKs
